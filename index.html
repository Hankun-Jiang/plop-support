About Plop

Plop helps you log your dog's poops in seconds — consistency, color, a photo, a rating, the time, and where it happened — then see it all as a journal and on a map. It's a warm, private little health diary that makes it easy to spot the changes a vet would want to know about. Track as many dogs as you like.

Common questions

Do I need an account to use Plop?

No. Plop works fully offline and your data stays on your device. Cloud backup is optional — turn it on in Settings with just your email if you want your entries to sync to a new phone.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings → Back Up My Data and enter your email. We'll send a secure sign-in link. Once backed up, signing in on a new device restores everything.

Why does Plop ask for location or photos?

Both are optional. Location tags where an entry happened so it can appear on your map; photos let you attach a picture to an entry. Deny either and the app still works.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Clear All Entries removes your logs. If you set up a backup, Delete Account removes your cloud data too, and you can choose to erase everything on the device.

Privacy

Plop is private by default. Your data lives on your device unless you choose to turn on cloud backup. We don't show ads and we don't sell your data. If you enable backup, your entries and email are stored securely so you can restore them on a new device.
